# Code of conduct

This project is a place to argue about evidence, not about people.

## Expected

- Criticize claims, gates and evidence as hard as you like; that is the point
  of the project. Do not criticize the person who made them.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when you think something is wrong.
- Accept an `INSUFFICIENT` on your own work the way you would give one.
- Keep discussion understandable to someone who was not there: state the claim,
  show the raw result.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Personal attacks, including on someone's competence or motives.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules,
and may block repeat or serious offenders from the project. To report a
problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/winterbim) or through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/winterbim/wincreator/security/advisories/new)
if no address is listed. Reports are handled confidentially.
